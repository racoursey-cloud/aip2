# Editorial standard

**PLACEHOLDER — to be re-authored in Wave 1**, from the research conclusions, in our own words,
with zero third-party text. The research corpus that produced these conclusions stays in the
sealed archive and never comes near this repository.

The standard has to carry at least these, all of which the research paid for:

## The access doctrine

Observed material or an honest hole — **never invent**. If nobody saw it, the piece says nobody
saw it. A missing quote is a hole in the piece, not a licence to write one.

## Marking

Every claim in a piece is one of four things, and the standard says which:

| mark | means |
|---|---|
| **fact** | checkable against a table or a public record |
| **reported** | someone else reported it; attributed, and the attribution is load-bearing |
| **lore** | the received story; flagged as received, never laundered into fact |
| **observed** | somebody watched it happen and said so, with a timestamp |

`site.observations` is the anti-hallucination feed: scene gets written only from what a human
actually saw.

## Form

The form rules — what a pregame piece owes the reader, what a postgame piece owes, and what the
notebook is for — get written here in Wave 1, along with the self-grading rubric the pieces are
