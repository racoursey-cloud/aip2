# Operating charter v2

**PLACEHOLDER — to be re-authored in Wave 1.** Charter v1 lives in the sealed archive
(`claude/operating-charter.md` in the previous project's `docs.documents`). This file exists so
the charter has a home in the new record from the first commit; its text is deliberately not
carried over verbatim, because v2 is written against the new footing, not the old one.

What v2 has to say, at minimum:

- **The three roles.** The Publisher rules, spends, brands, observes, and signs every piece.
  The planner plans, assembles, edits, and proposes. The builder builds.
- **The mode gate** — DISCOVERY / BUILD / RULING — and what each mode may and may not do.
- **Ratification is a pull-request approval.** The Publisher's review on the diff is the gate.
- **Infrastructure clicks are the Publisher's** — projects, repositories, spend, credentials —
  even when the spend is pre-approved. "Help" means walk him through it.
- **The one law**: nothing rights-flagged and no secret enters this repository.

The working rules that back these — eleven of them, each bought by a named incident — are
seeded into `ops.working_rules` by migration `0001` and are queryable there.
